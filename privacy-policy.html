Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Full Throttle (“the Game,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you play the Full Throttle mobile game. It applies to the game and its online multiplayer features.

1. Information the Game handles

Information stored on your device. The Game stores gameplay progress, unlocks, selected vehicles, game settings, and other preferences locally on your device using browser or app storage. This information is used to operate the Game and is not automatically sent to us just because it is stored locally.

Multiplayer information. If you use online multiplayer, the Game uses Firebase Realtime Database to operate rooms and synchronize gameplay. Depending on how you use multiplayer, information may include:

  • Your chosen guest display name (which may be a randomly generated guest name if you do not enter one).
  • A game-generated player identifier.
  • Room codes, room membership, host and ready status, and timestamps.
  • Race-related information such as selected track, player progress, finish status, and other game state needed to synchronize a match.
  • Messages you choose to send through the in-game multiplayer chat.

The current game does not require you to create an account with an email address to use guest multiplayer. Do not include personal or sensitive information in your display name or chat messages.

2. Advertising

The Android version may display interstitial or other advertisements through Google AdMob or another advertising service configured by the app distribution/building environment. When ads are enabled, the advertising provider may collect or receive information such as your device or advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, app interaction and ad events, and diagnostic information. The provider uses this information to deliver, measure, limit, and help protect advertising, subject to its own privacy terms and your device or Google advertising settings.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on the configuration, applicable law, and available consent or device settings. We do not control the data practices of third-party advertising providers.

Google's information about how it handles data in its advertising services is available at Google Advertising Policies and Technologies.

3. Third-party services

The Game uses Google Firebase Realtime Database for online multiplayer functionality. Firebase may process the multiplayer information described above on our behalf to provide database hosting, synchronization, and related services. Google's Firebase privacy information is available at Firebase Privacy and Security and Google Privacy Policy.

If advertising is enabled, Google AdMob or the advertising provider configured for the app may process advertising-related information as described in Section 2.

4. How information is used

  • To save your local game progress and preferences.
  • To create, join, operate, and synchronize multiplayer rooms and races.
  • To deliver multiplayer chat and display player names and race status.
  • To serve and measure advertisements when advertising is enabled.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Game.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Multiplayer information is made available to other participants in the relevant game room when needed to operate that room (for example, guest name, readiness, race status, and chat messages). Information may also be processed by the service providers described in this policy, including Firebase and advertising providers, to provide their services. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Game, its users, or others.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data generally remains on your device until you clear the Game's app/browser storage or uninstall the app (device and app behavior may vary). Multiplayer room and chat data may remain in the Firebase database for as long as it is needed to operate the service or until it is deleted or overwritten. We do not promise a fixed automatic deletion period for multiplayer records.

You can stop using multiplayer at any time. To ask about access to or deletion of multiplayer information associated with a guest name or room, contact us at the email below and include the relevant guest name and room code if available. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

7. Security

We use third-party infrastructure, including Firebase, to provide online features. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Avoid sharing personal information in multiplayer names or chat.

8. Children's privacy

Full Throttle is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through multiplayer, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate steps. Where local law sets a higher age or additional requirements, those rules apply.

9. Your choices

  • You can choose not to use online multiplayer.
  • You can use a non-identifying guest name and avoid sharing personal information in chat.
  • You can clear local app storage or uninstall the Game to remove locally stored game data, subject to your device's behavior.
  • You can manage advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account where available.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game or its services change. The revised policy will be posted at this same URL with an updated effective date. Please review it periodically.
